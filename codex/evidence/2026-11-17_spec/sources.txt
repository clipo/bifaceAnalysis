https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1525-142X.2009.00347.x
Klingenberg (2009), HTML sections 'The RV coefficient' and 'Permutation test of covariation'.
RV uses squared cross-covariances normalized by within-block covariance magnitudes.
For simultaneous Procrustes fits, the article distinguishes permutations with a new joint fit from permutations of frozen coordinates. The former preserve the alignment contribution in a reference for independence of parts.

https://morphometrics.uk/MorphoJ_guide/covariation/pls_intraconfig.htm
Official guide, Background and Text output. Joint fitting induces interdependence. The permutation procedure includes a new fit. RV is analogous to squared correlation, and joint-fit results include relative sizes and arrangement of parts.
